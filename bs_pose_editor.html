Points in room coordinates (the X / Y / Z of the ODOMETRY panel). From the ground press INIZIO
      (it takes off by itself), or take off by hand, switch to GUIDED on the radio and press Start.
      Any other mode on the radio (Loiter, PosHold) stops the mission at once.

min. stations seeing it

      
INIZIO does everything from the ground: 3 s countdown (Stop cancels), GUIDED, arm, vertical
        climb, hover, then the points (relative points start from the hover point). Best where 3 or more stations
        see it (on the floor the height can be wrong and jump at lift-off), keep the radio in hand — any mode change takes over — and stay clear
        of the propellers. If the take-off fails on the ground it disarms; in the air it hovers.

TRACK · FLIGHT LOG & ACCURACY

    
Records every lighthouse position (each ODOMETRY) with the EKF position, attitude, battery, mode and the
      mission target. Typical study: Record, take off, switch to GUIDED with a one-point mission and a long hold
      (Mission → Hold, e.g. 120 s), then Stop and look at how the samples spread around the point.
